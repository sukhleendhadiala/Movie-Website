Recommendations

Thriller

Shutter Island (2010)

Shutter Island is a psychological thriller about U.S. Marshal Teddy Daniels, who investigates a mysterious disappearance at a remote mental institution. As the investigation deepens, eerie events and hidden truths blur the line between reality and illusion.

Fractured (2019)

Fractured is a psychological thriller about a man who rushes his family to the hospital after an accident, only for things to take a strange turn. As confusion and tension build, he becomes determined to uncover the truth behind what’s really happening.

Inception (2010)

Inception is a mind-bending thriller that follows a skilled thief who specializes in stealing secrets by entering people's dreams. Tasked with a high-stakes mission, he and his team navigate layered dreamscapes where the boundaries between reality and imagination blur.

10 Things I Hate About You (1999)

10 Things I Hate About You is a witty teen comedy that explores love, identity, and high school drama through the lives of two very different sisters. With sharp humor and heartfelt moments, the film navigates the challenges of dating, independence, and staying true to yourself.

Romance

Pride and Prejudice (2005)

Pride and Prejudice is a classic romance that follows the strong-willed Elizabeth Bennet as she navigates societal expectations, family pressures, and unexpected connections in 19th-century England. Through sharp dialogue and keen observations, the story explores themes of love, class, and personal growth.

Sleepless in Seattle (1993)

Sleepless in Seattle is a heartfelt romantic drama about two strangers brought together by fate and a late-night radio show. As their lives unfold on opposite sides of the country, an unexpected connection begins to form in the most serendipitous way.

Bullet Train (2022)

Bullet Train is a high-octane action thriller that follows a group of skilled assassins aboard a fast-moving train in Japan, each with their own mission and secrets. As chaos unfolds, their paths collide in unexpected and explosive ways, blending action with sharp humor and stylish storytelling.

The Man From Toronto (2022)

The Man from Toronto is an action-comedy that follows a case of mistaken identity when an ordinary man is confused for a deadly assassin. As he's dragged into a high-stakes mission, chaos, comedy, and unexpected teamwork unfold in wild and unpredictable ways.

School of Rock (2003)

School of Rock is a feel-good comedy about a passionate musician who finds himself teaching at a prestigious prep school. With a love for rock music and a rebellious spirit, he inspires his students in unexpected and unforgettable ways.

Comedy

Baby Driver (2017)

Baby Driver is a stylish action film about a talented young getaway driver who relies on music to keep him focused behind the wheel. As he navigates high-stakes jobs and a chance at a new life, he finds himself caught between loyalty and freedom.

Dune (2021)

Dune is an epic sci-fi adventure that follows a young nobleman as he journeys to a dangerous desert planet at the center of a powerful struggle over a rare and valuable resource. As political tensions rise and ancient prophecies unfold, he must confront his destiny in a world of intrigue, survival, and transformation.

Action

Extraction (2020)

Extraction is an intense action thriller about a fearless mercenary who embarks on a high-risk mission to rescue a kidnapped child in a hostile city. As the stakes escalate, he battles overwhelming odds in a relentless fight for survival and redemption.
